I request reversal of the denial of the L4-L5 fusion for my patient, Mr. Gerald Whitcomb.
The denial cited Criteria 1 and 5 of Medical Policy MVP-SP-07. Your reviewer agreed that
Criteria 2, 3 and 4 are met. Both remaining criteria are also satisfied.

Criterion 1 - Instability. MVP-SP-07 requires translation of 4 mm or more, OR angular
motion of 10 degrees or more, on flexion-extension radiographs. The denial considered only
translation (3.5 mm). The same flexion-extension study dated {{FLEXEX|long}} documents
"sagittal angulation change at L4-5: 11 degrees between flexion and extension," and the
radiologist's impression is segmental motion "suggestive of instability" (Enclosure 1).
Eleven degrees exceeds the 10-degree threshold, so Criterion 1 is met.

Criterion 5 - Nicotine. Mr. Whitcomb stopped smoking on {{QUIT|long}}, more than six weeks
before the planned surgery, and has used no nicotine replacement since his first three
weeks. His cotinine test is negative (Enclosure 2), confirming abstinence as the policy
requires.

For completeness: Mr. Whitcomb has 14 months of neurogenic claudication with an ODI of 48%
and walking tolerance of about one block. He failed 18 physical therapy visits, NSAIDs,
gabapentin and two epidural steroid injections, and his MRI shows severe L4-5 central
stenosis with grade I spondylolisthesis (Enclosures 3 and 4).

All five criteria of MVP-SP-07 are met. I request approval of the procedure and am
available for a peer-to-peer discussion.
