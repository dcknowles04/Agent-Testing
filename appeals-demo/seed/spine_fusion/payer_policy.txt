MERIDIAN VALLEY HEALTH PLAN
Medical Policy MVP-SP-07: Lumbar Spinal Fusion (Illustrative)

Lumbar fusion for degenerative spondylolisthesis is considered medically necessary when
ALL of the following are met:

Criterion 1 - Instability: spondylolisthesis with translation of 4 mm or more, OR angular
motion of 10 degrees or more, on flexion-extension radiographs.

Criterion 2 - Symptoms: neurogenic claudication or radiculopathy with functional
impairment (e.g., ODI of 40% or more) for at least 3 months.

Criterion 3 - Conservative care: at least 3 months of non-operative care in the past 12
months, including physical therapy plus one or more of: medications or epidural
injections.

Criterion 4 - Imaging correlation: MRI or CT demonstrating stenosis or neural compression
at the operative level.

Criterion 5 - Nicotine: for current or recent (past 6 months) tobacco users, nicotine
abstinence for at least 6 weeks before surgery, confirmed by a negative cotinine test.
