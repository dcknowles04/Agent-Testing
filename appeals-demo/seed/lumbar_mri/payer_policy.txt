CRESTVIEW MUTUAL HEALTH
Clinical Guideline CG-RAD-12: MRI of the Lumbar Spine (Illustrative)

Lumbar spine MRI (CPT 72148, 72149, 72158) is considered medically necessary for ONE of the
following indications:

Indication A - Persistent low back pain with or without radiculopathy after at least 6
weeks of documented conservative therapy in the past 6 months (e.g., physical therapy,
medication, activity modification), with continued symptoms, in a patient who is a
candidate for intervention.

Indication B - Red-flag findings, for which no conservative-therapy period is required:
  B1. Progressive or severe neurological deficit (e.g., new or worsening motor weakness);
  B2. Suspected cauda equina syndrome;
  B3. Suspected infection, malignancy or fracture.

Indication C - Pre-operative or pre-procedural planning (e.g., epidural steroid injection
or surgery) once a procedure is being actively considered.

Documentation requirements: Office notes must describe symptom duration, conservative
treatments and their dates, and the neurological examination. When Indication B1 is used,
the notes should document the progression (comparison of serial examinations).
