SUMMIT HEALTH PLAN
Medical Policy MP-ORTHO-031: Total Knee Arthroplasty (Illustrative)
Effective: 01/01 of current plan year

Summit Health Plan considers primary total knee arthroplasty (CPT 27447) medically
necessary when ALL of the following criteria are met:

Criterion 1 - Imaging. Weight-bearing radiographs within the past 12 months demonstrate
advanced arthritis: Kellgren-Lawrence grade 3 or 4, OR bone-on-bone articulation, OR
severe joint space narrowing.

Criterion 2 - Function. Moderate to severe pain and functional impairment interfering with
activities of daily living for at least 3 months, documented with a validated outcome
measure (e.g., KOOS JR, WOMAC) or a detailed functional description.

Criterion 3 - Conservative care. Failure of at least 12 weeks of non-operative treatment
within the past 12 months, including at least TWO of the following:
  a) supervised physical therapy or a documented home exercise program;
  b) NSAIDs or other analgesics, unless contraindicated;
  c) intra-articular injection (corticosteroid or viscosupplement);
  d) weight-loss counseling when BMI is 30 or greater; activity modification or an
     assistive device.
Documentation must include dates and response to treatment. Physical therapy must be
supported by therapy notes or a discharge summary.

Criterion 4 - BMI below 40, or a documented optimization plan.

Criterion 5 - No active local or systemic infection; for patients with diabetes, HbA1c
below 8.0% within the past 3 months.

Criterion 6 - Documented shared decision-making between the surgeon and the patient.
