PINNACLE CARE ALLIANCE
Reimbursement Policy RP-114: Arthroscopic Knee Procedures (Illustrative)

Section 3 - Bundled services. Pinnacle applies NCCI procedure-to-procedure (PTP) edits.
Limited synovectomy (29875) and chondroplasty (29877) are included in the meniscectomy
codes 29880/29881 and are not separately reimbursed.

Section 4 - Major synovectomy (29876) with meniscectomy. CPT 29876 is eligible for separate
reimbursement with 29880/29881 only when ALL of the following apply:
  4.1 the synovectomy is performed in compartment(s) separate from the compartment where
      the meniscectomy was performed;
  4.2 the operative report specifically documents the separate compartment(s) treated;
  4.3 the claim line is billed with modifier XS (separate structure) or 59, and the
      laterality modifier (RT/LT).

Section 5 - Corrected claims. Corrected claims (frequency code 7) and appeals must be
received within 90 days of the original remittance date. Include the operative report
when billing modifier XS or 59.
