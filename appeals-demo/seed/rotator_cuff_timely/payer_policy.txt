*** ILLUSTRATIVE POLICY - FICTIONAL PAYER - NOT A REAL PROVIDER MANUAL ***

HARBORVIEW HEALTH PARTNERS
Provider Manual, Section 7: Claims Submission (Illustrative)

7.1 Filing limit. Participating providers must submit clean claims so that they are
received by Harborview within 90 days of the date of service.

7.4 Proof of timely filing. A timely filing denial will be overturned when the provider
submits ONE of the following showing receipt within the filing limit:
  a) a Harborview 277CA claim acknowledgment or a Harborview-issued claim number;
  b) a clearinghouse payer-connection / acceptance report showing the claim was
     transmitted to AND accepted by Harborview (not only accepted by the clearinghouse);
  c) Harborview correspondence (e.g., a request for records) that references the claim.
A 999 acknowledgment or a clearinghouse-level acceptance alone is NOT sufficient.

7.6 Exceptions. Harborview may waive the filing limit for documented Harborview system or
connectivity outages affecting claim receipt, upon review of supporting evidence.
