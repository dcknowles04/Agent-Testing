Aetna CPB 0016 – Trigger Point Injections**
Aetna CPB 0016 states:
“Trigger point injections are considered medically necessary for the treatment of myofascial pain syndrome when all of the following criteria are met:
- There is a regional pain complaint in the expected distribution of referral pain from a trigger point;
- There is spot tenderness in a palpable taut band in a muscle;
- There is restricted range of motion; and
- Conservative therapy (e.g., physical therapy, oral analgesics, activity modification) has failed.

The submitted medical records EXPLICITLY AND FULLY documents each of these required elements, including cervical regional pain, palpable taut bands with focal tenderness, restricted cervical motion, and failure of conservative management.

“Trigger point injections may be repeated if the prior injections provided significant relief of pain and improvement in function.”**
